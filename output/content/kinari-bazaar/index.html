Market overview

A slim passageway is a key player in Delhi's thriving wedding sector.

Located in the heart of Shahjahanabad's Chandni Chowk, Kinari Bazaar is surrounded by Paranthe Wali Gali, Dariba Kalan, Nai Sarak, and Gurudwara Sis Ganj Sahib. The market's name, 'kinari,' reflects its focus on opulent trimmings such as gold and silver borders, embroidered ribbons, brocade, mirrors, sequins, beads, tassels, and other embellishments sought after by boutique owners, tailors, event planners, artisans, and families preparing for memorable events.

Source borders, appliques, lace, gota and embroidery material.

Travelers

Experience one of Old Delhi’s most colorful specialty bazaars.

What to buy in Kinari Bazaar

For a thorough market exploration, consider shopping by category. Despite their compact appearance, shops typically boast a wide range of items tucked away in every nook, so it's wise to bring along reference photos, fabric swatches, and measurements.

Textile trims

  • Zari, brocade, gota patti and metallic borders
  • Crochet lace, net lace, velvet trims and piping
  • Mirror-work, bead-work and sequin strips
  • Decorative patches for lehengas, sarees and dupattas

Wedding accessories

  • Latkans, tassels, kalire-style hangings and dori ends
  • Shagun envelopes, potli bags and ring platters
  • Decorated trays, thalis, boxes and return-gift packaging
  • Invitation embellishments and craft supplies

Festival and pooja decor

  • Idol poshak trims, tiny garlands and decorative fabrics
  • Diwali torans, rakhi material and seasonal craft pieces
  • Artificial flowers, ribbons and ornate packaging

Fashion add-ons

  • Imitation jewellery, hair accessories and decorative clips
  • Beads, buttons and stones for DIY embellishment
  • Bulk craft material for boutiques and online sellers

Plan your visit

LocationSituated close to Paranthe Wali Gali, Dariba Kalan, and Gurudwara Sis Ganj Sahib in Old Delhi, just a stone's throw
MetroChandni Chowk station serves as the main access point for the Yellow Line, with Lal Qila and Chawri Bazar stations also offering alternative routes depending on your preferred walking path.
TimingsStores are usually open from 10 or 11 AM to 8 PM, but hours may vary by shop.
ClosedSeveral lanes and shops in Chandni Chowk and Kinari Bazaar usually shut down on Sundays, although a few may have varying opening times.
Best timeWeekday mornings are optimal for focused shopping, with October to March offering the most pleasant conditions for walking.
Entry feeEntry is free; you only pay for shopping, food, rickshaw rides, or guided walks.

2026 visitor context

Chandni Chowk's primary section has been redesigned to focus on pedestrian traffic, but issues such as heavy foot traffic, encroachments, and ongoing upkeep still affect the area. Delhi is enhancing its infrastructure with initiatives like underground cabling in Chandni Chowk, so anticipate occasional disruptions at the street level and plan for additional time when walking.

Smart shopping tips

Carry swatches and measurements

When buying borders and lace, be sure to know the needed length and bring the fabric color along as store lighting can change shades.

Bargain, but compare first

Prices may vary depending on the quality, width, quantity, and finish. It is important to compare prices at multiple stores before making a large purchase.

Use cash plus UPI

Although digital payments are accepted by most stores, smaller vendors and less expensive items may find cash to be more convenient.

Travel light

The lanes are cramped and crowded, so choose a sling bag, comfortable shoes, and avoid bringing bulky luggage.
